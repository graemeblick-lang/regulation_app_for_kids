# Privacy Policy for Calm Corner

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Calm Corner is a calming-skills app for children and young people. It is built to be private by design.

**We do not collect any personal information.** The app:
- has no accounts or sign-in,
- does not use the internet (it has no network permission),
- contains no ads, analytics or third-party tracking,
- does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Any settings you choose stay on your device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

If this ever changes, we will update this policy and the app's store listing before releasing the change.
