## Calm Corner v0.3.0

Install on an Android phone: tap **CalmCorner-v0.3.0.apk** below, then open it. If you already have Calm Corner, it installs over the top.

### What's in this version
**Check-in loop.** Pick a colour (Blue, Green, Yellow or Red), get suggested tools, then "How do you feel now?". Every finished tool earns a ⭐.

**Tools (13):** Box Breathing, Finger Breathing, Balloon Breathing, Five Senses, Squeeze and Relax, Calm Jar, Bubble Pop, Calm Drum, Heartbeat Drum, Body Moves, Trace and Breathe, Calm Sounds, Calm Timer.

**New in 0.3.0**
- **My calm plan:** star favourite tools; grown-ups add her own calm ideas with photos; a calm picture shows when she picks Red.
- **New tools:** Body Moves, Trace and Breathe, Heartbeat Drum, Calm Sounds.
- **Body clues:** "How does Red feel in my body?" with a picture and simple words.
- **Grown-ups area:** share a weekly summary PDF, school mode (silent), daily check-in reminders, reduce movement, bigger text and buttons.
- Screen-reader labels on all animated pictures.

**Also included:** 10 voice choices, glossy bubbles with pop sound, wooden drum, literal wording throughout, home-screen widget, and no internet, accounts, ads or tracking.
